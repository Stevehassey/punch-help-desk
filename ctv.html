03 — CTV

CTV (Connected TV)

Streaming TV — think Hulu, Peacock, or a smart TV app — bought and measured more like digital than traditional broadcast.

CTV vs. OTT vs. Linear TV — Linear TV is traditional, scheduled broadcast or cable — what's "on" at a set time. OTT ("over-the-top") means any video streamed over the internet instead of through a cable box. CTV is OTT content specifically watched on an actual television screen — a smart TV, Roku, Apple TV, or game console — as opposed to a phone or laptop.

Addressable TV — The ability to show two different households two different ads during the exact same TV show, based on data about each household. Traditional TV with a layer of digital-style targeting.

ACR (Automatic Content Recognition) Data — Information a smart TV collects about what's actually playing on screen, used to help measure and target CTV ads.

Household-Level vs. Device-Level Targeting — CTV usually targets a whole household (one TV, several possible viewers), not one specific device or person — which limits how precise the targeting and measurement can really be.

VOD (Video on Demand) — Content you choose to watch whenever you want, instead of at a scheduled time.

Live vs. On-Demand Inventory — Ad space during a live event (a game, an awards show) is priced and sold differently than ad space in on-demand shows and movies.

Publisher-Direct vs. Programmatic CTV — You can buy CTV ad space directly from a specific streaming app, or buy it programmatically alongside the rest of your digital plan. Each publisher-direct platform (YouTube, Hulu, Roku, and others) runs its own political ad verification process — plan for days to weeks of lead time, not same-day setup.

FCC Rules Don't Apply Here — CTV and streaming are internet-delivered, so they fall outside the FCC's broadcast rules — no "lowest unit rate" pricing window, no equal-opportunity requirement, no reasonable-access rule the way broadcast and cable have. That said, FEC disclaimer requirements ("paid for by," and "I approve this message" for federal candidates) still apply regardless of platform, and a growing number of states have their own additional disclosure rules for streaming/digital specifically. State rules are changing quickly right now — confirm current requirements with your Punch contact or legal team rather than assuming last cycle's rules still hold.

How-To

Why is CTV buying different from traditional TV buying? — CTV gives you TV's reach and creative impact, combined with digital's targeting and reporting detail. But it also inherits some real TV-sized headaches — it's harder to control how many times one household sees your ad across different apps and devices, and measurement tools are still catching up to what digital advertisers are used to.
