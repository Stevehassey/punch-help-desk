Worth Knowing About

Cool Digital Ideas

A running list of interesting ad formats and platform features — not case studies of specific campaigns, just things worth knowing exist.

Shoppable Video — Video ads with clickable product tags built right in, so someone can buy without ever leaving the video. Increasingly common on social and CTV alike.

Interactive CTV Ads — Ads on streaming TV that let a viewer use their remote to request more info, grab a coupon, or save something for later — without leaving the show they're watching.

Dynamic Creative Optimization (DCO) — Technology that automatically assembles many versions of an ad — swapping images, copy, or offers based on who's seeing it — instead of one static version running for everyone.

Vertical Video — Video shot and edited specifically for phone screens (full-screen, top-to-bottom) rather than a horizontal TV or YouTube video squeezed to fit. Standard now on TikTok, Reels, and Stories.

Retail Media Placements — Ads that run directly on a retailer's own website or app — think a sponsored product in search results on a shopping site — reaching someone at the exact moment they're already looking to buy.

AI-Generated Creative Variations — Platforms increasingly offer tools that automatically generate multiple versions of an ad's text or imagery to test what resonates, rather than a human manually building every version by hand.

Audio Ads on Streaming Platforms — Ads that run between songs or podcast episodes on services like Spotify — targetable in a lot of the same ways as other digital ads, just heard instead of seen.

Gamified / Interactive Display Ads — Banner-style ads that let someone play a quick mini-game, spin a wheel, or swipe through options right inside the ad itself, instead of just looking at a static image.

This page is just an idea list — whether any of these make sense for your brand is a strategy conversation.
