Working With Punch

Process FAQ

The practical questions that come up before and during a campaign. For anything account-specific, your Punch contact will have the exact details.

How long does it take to launch a campaign?

It depends on the channel and how much needs to be built from scratch — accounts, tracking, creative, approvals. Once everything's in place, many campaigns can go live within a couple of weeks; a brand-new CTV or programmatic setup can take longer. Your Punch contact can give you a specific timeline once they know your plan.

What do you need from us to get started?

Generally: access to (or help creating) your ad accounts, approved creative, brand guidelines, and access to add tracking to your website. The Launch Checklist covers the full list.

How often will we get reports, and what's in them?

Cadence is usually weekly or monthly, depending on the campaign — confirm with your Punch contact. Reports typically cover spend and pacing, key performance metrics, and any notable changes worth flagging.

Who's our point of contact once we're live?

Your dedicated Punch contact stays your main point of contact throughout — for questions, changes, and reporting.

Can we make changes after a campaign launches?

Yes — campaigns are actively managed and adjusted based on performance. Bigger changes (budget, timeline, audience, creative) should go through your Punch contact so they can be planned properly rather than made on the fly.

What if we need to pause or end a campaign early?

Usually possible, though notice periods and any minimum commitments depend on your specific agreement. Check with your Punch contact or your contract for the exact terms.

Do we need a long-term contract?

This varies by engagement. Ask your Punch contact about the options that make sense for your situation.

How is budget or billing typically structured?

Agencies commonly work on some combination of media spend plus a management fee, or a flat retainer — the right structure depends on your goals and budget. Your Punch contact can walk through what applies to your account.

Do you handle creative, or do we need our own?

Depends on the engagement — some clients bring their own creative, others want support building it. Worth a direct conversation with your Punch contact about what's included.
