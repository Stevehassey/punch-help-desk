Reference

Creative Specs

The formats we run most often: horizontal 16:9 in :15 and :30 for video and CTV, plus Meta's newsfeed and 9:16 vertical placements. Specs shift over time and some platforms vary by publisher, so confirm with your Punch contact before locking picture.

YouTube (Google Ads)

Horizontal 16:9, :15 and :30

SpecDetail
Aspect ratio16:9 (horizontal)
Resolution1920×1080 recommended (1280×720 minimum)
DurationSkippable in-stream works at either :15 or :30, with a skip button appearing at 5 seconds. Non-skippable in-stream is generally capped at :15–:20; a :30 non-skippable format exists specifically on YouTube TV/Select and must match that exact duration to serve.
File formatMP4 (H.264) recommended; MOV and AVI are also accepted
Max file sizeVery generous (Google states up to 256 GB) — not a practical constraint for typical creative
AudioAAC or MP3, 44.1 kHz or higher

Which duration and format (skippable vs. non-skippable, in-stream vs. YouTube TV) is right depends on the plan; confirm with your Punch contact before production.

Meta

Newsfeed and 9:16 vertical (Stories & Reels)

Newsfeed

SpecDetail
Aspect ratio1:1 is the safest universal option for feed; 4:5 often performs better on mobile since it takes up more screen
Resolution1080×1080 (1:1) or 1080×1350 (4:5)
File formatMP4 or MOV (H.264)
Max file sizeUp to 4 GB, though keeping well under 1 GB is recommended for smoother delivery
DurationTechnically anywhere from 1 second to 240 minutes; :15–:30 is the practical range for this kind of ad

Vertical, 9:16 (Stories & Reels)

SpecDetail
Aspect ratio9:16, full-screen
Resolution1080×1920 recommended
File formatMP4 or MOV (H.264)
Max file sizeSame as feed: up to 4 GB, aim well under 1 GB
DurationReels cap at 90 seconds; :15–:30 is the sweet spot

Keep key text, logos, and captions out of the top and bottom ~14% of a 9:16 frame; that space is where the profile name, caption, and CTA overlays sit.

Programmatic: Freewheel

Horizontal 16:9, :15 and :30

Freewheel isn't one platform with a single fixed spec sheet: it's the ad-serving layer behind many premium streaming publishers (Hulu, NBCUniversal/Peacock, and others), and actual bitrate and file requirements can vary a lot by which specific publisher the buy is running on. Treat the numbers below as a starting point, not a final spec.

SpecDetail
Aspect ratio16:9
Resolution1920×1080
Duration:15 and :30
File formatMP4 (H.264)
BitrateVaries significantly by publisher: commonly 1,200–1,500 kbps minimum, though some premium publishers require much higher (Hulu-hosted inventory, for example, has required upward of 15,000 kbps)
Max file sizeNo single fixed cap: generally calculated from duration × bitrate rather than a flat limit

Confirm exact bitrate and file requirements with your Punch contact for the specific publisher(s) in the plan before sending a file to production.
