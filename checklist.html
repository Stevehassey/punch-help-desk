Launch Checklist

Getting a campaign ready to go live.

Before anything goes live, these are the pieces that need to be in place. None of this replaces a conversation with your Punch team — think of it as the checklist you're both working from.

01

Accounts & Access

  • Ad accounts are created, with the right people (agency and client) given access.
  • A payment method or billing terms are confirmed on each account.
  • Political/election ad advertiser verification or certification is complete on every platform being used (Meta, Google, X, Snapchat, and any DSPs) — see the Social and Search pages. This step alone can take days to weeks, so start it early.
  • The "Paid for by" disclaimer text is finalized and approved — it needs to be ready before any ad can go live, not added after.

02

Tracking & Measurement

  • The tracking pixel (or Conversions API) is installed on the website and confirmed to be firing correctly.
  • The key actions that matter for this campaign — a donation, a volunteer sign-up, an email capture — are tagged so they can be measured.
  • Every link in the campaign has tracking tags (UTMs) added, so results can be tied back to the right campaign and ad.

03

Targeting & Audience

  • The target audience is defined — who you're trying to reach, where, and anyone you want to exclude.
  • Whichever targeting methods the plan relies on (audience lists, lookalikes, geofencing) have been confirmed as actually available for political ads on that specific platform — several are restricted or disabled. See the Ad Basics page.
  • A frequency cap is set, so the same person isn't shown the ad too many times.

04

Creative

  • Final creative is approved and delivered in the exact sizes and formats each platform requires.
  • The "Paid for by" disclaimer is present on the creative itself, not just the account — required on Meta, X, and Snapchat, and separately required for federal candidate ads under FEC rules.
  • If any creative used generative AI, disclosure requirements for that have been checked on each platform being used.
  • Every link in the ad has been clicked and tested to make sure it goes to the right place.
  • Legal sign-off on creative and disclaimers is in hand for your specific jurisdiction — state rules on this are changing quickly.

05

Budget & Timing

  • Total budget, and any daily or weekly budget limits, are confirmed.
  • Start and end dates are set correctly — and the time zone has been double-checked.
  • Everyone agrees on what "on pace" looks like, so it's easy to spot early if something's off.

06

Final Checks

  • A small test or preview run has happened, if the platform allows one.
  • Everyone who needs to sign off has signed off.
  • There's a shared understanding of what success looks like for this campaign, and when the first report will land.

Something on this list unclear?

If anything here feels vague or specific to your account, that's exactly what your Punch contact is for.
