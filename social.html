05 — Social

Social

Meta, TikTok, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Snap, X — different apps, but the underlying setup is more similar than you'd think.

Account Structure — Most platforms organize things in layers. On Meta, it's Campaign → Ad Set → Ad. On Google and TikTok, it's generally Campaign → Ad Group → Ad. Budget and targeting usually live in the middle layer; the actual ad creative sits at the bottom.

Pixel / Conversions API — The Pixel is a small piece of code that tracks what visitors do on your website. The Conversions API sends that same kind of information a different way — directly from your server — which has become the more reliable option as browser tracking has gotten less dependable.

Custom Audience vs. Lookalike Audience — A custom audience is built from your own data — your customer list, your website visitors. A lookalike audience is a brand-new group the platform builds because it resembles that custom audience.

Boosted Post vs. Paid Ad — "Boosting" takes an existing post and promotes it with a few simplified settings — quick, but limited. A properly built paid ad gives you full control over targeting, placement, and how much you bid. Easier isn't the same as more effective.

Algorithm-Driven Optimization (e.g., Advantage+, Smart+) — Newer campaign types that hand more of the decision-making — targeting, placement, sometimes even which creative to show whom — over to the platform's own automation. You give up some manual control in exchange for (often) better results with less work.

Basic Creative Specs — Every platform has its own rules for image size, video length, and text limits — and they change from time to time. Always check current requirements before finalizing an ad; don't rely on what you remember from last time.

How-To

How to get verified on Meta — Verification (for a Business Manager or Page) happens through Meta Business Suite — you'll need your legal business name, address, phone number, and a supporting document like a business license or utility bill. Then Meta reviews it. If it gets stuck, or the requirements seem off, that's worth a quick check-in with your Punch contact rather than guessing at a fix.
How to set up a Meta Business Manager — Create the account at business.facebook.com, add your business's Page(s) and ad account(s), then assign access to your team. Everything else — verification, the Pixel, ad accounts — gets built on top of this.
How to install the Meta Pixel / Conversions API — In simple terms: the Pixel is a small piece of code added to your website (often through a tag manager) that fires when someone takes a key action. The Conversions API is its behind-the-scenes counterpart, set up through Business Manager. This part is genuinely technical and specific to your website — bring in your dev team and your Punch contact rather than trying to do it from a wiki page.
How to set up a TikTok Business Center — Same basic idea as Meta's Business Manager — one central place to manage ad accounts, creative assets, and team access, set up through TikTok's own portal.
How to set up LinkedIn Campaign Manager access — Requires a LinkedIn Page first, then you assign account access to your team members inside Campaign Manager.

Political / issue ad advertiser authorization — This isn't one-size-fits-all — check in with your Punch contact before moving forward.
